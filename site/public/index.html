WLYL Parent

Your child's school day, in one app

WLYL Parent is the parent app for schools that use the We Learn You Learn (WLYL) school platform. Sign in with the email or phone number your school registered, and see:

  • Attendance, day by day
  • Fees due, payments and receipts
  • Exam schedules and mark sheets
  • The class timetable and school calendar
  • Notices from the school
  • Library textbooks to read offline

Your login comes from your school. If you don't have one, please ask the school office.
