Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy covers two Android apps for schools that use the We Learn You Learn (WLYL) school platform:

  • WLYL Parent (com.welearnyoulearn.wlyl), which lets parents see their child's school information.
  • WLYL Teacher (com.welearnyoulearn.teacher), which lets teachers take attendance, enter marks and manage their classes.

Questions: thindaam.ai@gmail.com.

Who holds the school records

The school enters and owns its records: students, classes, attendance, fees, exam marks, syllabus, timetable, calendar and notices. They are stored on the WLYL platform. Each app only shows a person the records the school has given them access to.

WLYL Parent: what the app collects and why

  • Sign-in details. Your email address or phone number and your password, sent to the WLYL platform to sign you in. The app does not store your password. After sign-in, your phone's operating system keeps a session cookie so you stay signed in.
  • Your parent profile. Your name, email and phone number, plus the children linked to you, as recorded by the school.
  • Things you submit. Payment references (for example a UPI transaction ID) when you report a fee payment, acknowledgements of exam marks, and leave requests, where your school uses these features. They go to your school through the WLYL platform.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a push token for your device, so the school can send you notices. It is removed when you sign out.

On your phone, the app keeps which child you last selected and any library textbooks you download for offline reading. Textbooks are deleted when you sign out.

WLYL Teacher: what the app collects and why

  • Sign-in details. The email address and password your school gave you, sent to the WLYL platform to sign you in. The app does not store your password. After sign-in, your phone's operating system keeps a session cookie so you stay signed in.
  • Your teacher profile. Your name, email, staff ID and the classes and subjects you teach, as recorded by the school. If you choose to add your date of birth, it is used only for a birthday greeting.
  • Work you enter. Attendance, exam marks, syllabus progress, replies to students' doubts and class messages. They go to your school through the WLYL platform, and the school decides who can see them.
  • Files you pick. When you upload marks from a CSV file or a syllabus from a JSON file, the app reads the file on your phone and sends only the values in it. The file itself is not uploaded.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a push token for your device, so you get notices about exams, marks, messages and attendance. It is removed when you sign out or when the school deactivates your account.

The Teacher app shows students' names, roll numbers, attendance and marks only for the classes the school has assigned to that teacher.

What neither app does

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools from other companies.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone other than the school.

Security

All data between the apps and the WLYL platform travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.

How long data is kept

A parent account is kept while the school uses WLYL and has that parent linked to a child. A teacher account is kept while the school keeps that teacher active. School records, including attendance and marks a teacher entered, are kept by the school under its own record-keeping rules.

Children

Both apps are for adults: parents and guardians, and school staff. They are not designed for children to use.
